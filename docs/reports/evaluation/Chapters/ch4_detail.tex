\evalhead{How matching works.} Recall is measured by keyword groups, so a question phrased with different words counts as a miss.

\evalhead{Review of the gold list.} On 14 November 2026 each of the 22 gold gaps was checked against the brief's scenario text, lines 77--111 of \code{data_pack/00_Brief_UPDATED.txt}. Every gap traces to a sentence there: for example G20 to ``about 100 children'', G21 to ``must remain open to residents throughout the works'', and G19 to ``becomes the estate's flagship shared space'' with no operator named. No gap was removed or added. The review was made with Claude and is owned by Qu Chengzhi.

\evalhead{Why both modes miss G20 and G21.} Neither the curated checklist nor the generated register raises them. Which other gaps the LLM misses changes from run to run: G14 and G19 in the final run, G12, G15 and G19 in run 3.
