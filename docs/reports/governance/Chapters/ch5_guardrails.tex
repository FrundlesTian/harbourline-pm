\chapter{Guardrail events}
\label{ch:guardrails}

Guardrails are the code-level interlocks that stop an agent, or a person, from taking an action that the governance design does not allow. They work like the circuit breakers and safety interlocks of production systems: they refuse the action and leave a trace, rather than relying on the caller to behave \parencite{nygard2018}. Table~\ref{tab:guardrails} lists each guardrail with its evidence, including the two rows for the start-screen Reset demo added with the 11 Nov change. Figure~\ref{fig:reset} shows the safeguards of the Reset demo.
