\chapter{Scope and sources of the records}
\label{ch:scope}

This Governance Log belongs to the Harbourline AI PM system of MA6094 Capstone Group 5. It is dated 14 November 2026 and is a draft for team review. The governance owner is Huang Hao, G2510626K; the team lead is Tian Yujia, G2510505D.

The system writes its governance records itself, into the SQLite tables \code{audit_log}, \code{approvals} and \code{handoffs}, see \code{app/db.py:58-64,81-83}. Keeping these records inside the system, rather than reconstructing them afterwards, follows the ``govern'' and ``map'' functions of AI risk management, which ask for documented accountability and traceable decisions \parencite{nist2023airmf}. Two kinds of record are reported here. Table~\ref{tab:sources} lists them, together with the team's real run on its Jira site.

\paragraph*{Reproduction and token hygiene.} The two evidence sets are reproduced with \texttt{python3 scripts/governance\_demo.py} and \texttt{python3 scripts/reset\_check.py}; see Appendix~\ref{app:repro}. The export contains no tokens: a scan for token patterns found 0 matches, and all log text passes through \code{audit.redact()} in \code{app/audit.py:33-40}.

\paragraph*{Structure of this log.} Chapter~\ref{ch:audit} reports the audit records of agent write operations. Chapter~\ref{ch:approvals} shows who approved each of the human checkpoints HC1 to HC7. Chapter~\ref{ch:bias} covers the bias and fairness check BIAS-1, Chapter~\ref{ch:guardrails} the guardrail events including the Reset demo, and Chapter~\ref{ch:incidents} the incidents I-01 to I-16 and the changes made. The appendices list the approval rows, the reset check results, the audit action counts, the reproduction commands and a summary of the read-only Jira snapshot.
