This log reports the governance records that the Harbourline AI PM system writes about itself into its SQLite tables \code{audit_log}, \code{approvals} and \code{handoffs}. The evidence is a scripted demonstration run on the latest code, in baseline mode against the mock board, with 188 audit rows, 137 agent writes and 10 approvals, plus a check of the start-screen Reset demo and a read-only snapshot of the team's Jira project. All 92 PM issues have a \texttt{pm create} row, and no agent wrote to the PM tool before checkpoints HC2 and HC3 were approved. Approvals were signed by seeded role accounts; they are demonstration decisions, not evidence of a real person's sign-off. Refused attempts were blocked and logged: a Group Leader approving HC2, release of a Critic-stopped draft, and agent close, delete and Done attempts. The bias and fairness check BIAS-1 found six skews in the 214 consultation responses, three of them high severity. The team carried out four of its five actions but not the second multilingual round for residents 55+, so the under-representation of residents 65+ remains. Of sixteen development incidents, thirteen are closed and I-04 is reopened. The two governance issues introduced by the 11 Nov change were accepted by the team lead: passwordless identity selection, under which the account no longer proves who approved, I-13, and a project-wide Reset demo that deletes every issue in the configured Jira project without a named session, I-14.
