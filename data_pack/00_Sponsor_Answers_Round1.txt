  Capstone Sponsor Answers — Round 1
    Oct 23, 2026        · ​@Spurthi

  Harbourline Community Hub Refurbishment. Answers from Daniel Koh, Director, Estate
  Development, Harbourline Town Council (played by the instructor). Questions came from
  one team; answers apply to every team.


  Q1. Does the 22-month timeline include tendering the design-and-
  build contract?
  No. The 22 months is construction only, counted from site possession.

       The design-and-build contract was awarded on 18 December 2026.
       Site possession is Monday 4 January 2027. Plan from that date.
       Tendering is not on your critical path. Design is, because the contractor designs and
       builds under the same contract.


  Q2. Who appoints the active ageing centre operator, and when?
  The Town Council appoints the operator through its own procurement, separate from the
  construction contract.

       An expression of interest will be issued in February 2027.
       Council procurement policy requires at least three responses before an appointment.
       There is no confirmed appointment date. Schedule and track it as a dependency.
       Check the grant conditions (data pack file 07) for anything that depends on this
       appointment.


  Q3. How can M&E be replaced if the Hub stays open, and what
  closes first?
  The Council's position is that the Hub remains open to residents throughout the works.

       The contractor is responsible for the phasing plan. No closures have been agreed yet.
       The Council will consider short, well-notified closures of individual areas if they are
       shown to be necessary.

       Whatever is closed, residents must keep barrier-free access to every floor in public
       use.

  The early childhood centre and active ageing centre are new facilities and do not exist yet,
  so they cannot close. The areas in use today are the sports hall, multi-purpose rooms,
  tuition centre and food court. The Council has not set a closure priority among them. If
  your plan needs one, state it as an assumption, show the impact of each option, and bring
  it to the sponsor as a decision.


  Q4. Does resident consultation happen before tender or during
  design and build?
  During design and build, because tendering is complete.

       The Council's Community Engagement team runs it, not the contractor.
       It runs 1 to 28 February 2027: online, paper forms at the Hub, and one town hall.
       The results go to the contractor as design input.

  Treat consultation as a Council work package whose summary feeds the contractor's
  design stage.


  Q5. Who funds the remaining 40%?
  The Town Council funds the balance from its own capital reserves.

       No other grant is being sought, so no fundraising work package is needed.
       The monthly report is a progress report to the grant body, not a funding request.
       Read the grant conditions (data pack file 07) carefully. The 60% applies to eligible cost,
       and what counts as eligible may change your funding picture.

  Any question the sponsor has not answered stays an open assumption in your register.
  Follow-up questions go through the CP2 round.
